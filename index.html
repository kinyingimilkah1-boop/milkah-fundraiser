01 / Why I am asking

Medicine is more than a career to me.

I have always been drawn to the moments when knowledge becomes comfort: a clear explanation, a steady hand, a patient who feels heard. Medical school is teaching me how to show up for those moments with real expertise.

The cost of staying enrolled, buying required resources, and getting to clinical placements has become more than I can carry alone. I am sharing my journey openly because I believe a community can make a lasting difference.
